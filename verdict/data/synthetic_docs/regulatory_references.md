# Regulatory References (synthetic stand-in)

REG-1 Suspicious Activity Reports: financial institutions must file a SAR for suspicious transactions involving $1,000 or more where the institution knows, suspects or has reason to suspect fraud (illustrative threshold for this exercise).
REG-2 SAR narrative must be complete, concise and chronological, covering who, what, when, where, why and how, and must not contain unsupported conclusions.
REG-3 Records supporting a SAR must be retained and be available to supervisors; decisions and approvals must be auditable.
REG-4 Customer communications about suspected fraud must not disclose that a SAR has been or will be filed (no tipping-off).
